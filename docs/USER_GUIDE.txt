RETAIL POS - USER GUIDE
=======================
Version 0.1.1. Shop software for Windows 10 / 11. Works fully offline.


1. INSTALLING ON WINDOWS
------------------------
1. Copy "Retail-POS-Setup-0.1.1.exe" to the shop PC (USB, WhatsApp, Google Drive).
2. Double-click it.
3. Windows may show a blue box "Windows protected your PC".
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway". (This happens because the
   installer is not digitally signed yet. It is safe.)
4. Choose the install folder (or keep the default) and click Install.
5. Open "Retail POS" from the desktop shortcut.

Your data is NOT stored in the install folder. It is kept in:
   C:\Users\<your name>\AppData\Roaming\Retail POS\
Uninstalling and installing again keeps all your data.


2. FIRST START (SETUP WIZARD)
-----------------------------
The first time the app opens, it asks for:
  - Shop name, address, phone (printed on receipts)
  - Language: English or Urdu
  - Receipt printer and paper width (58 mm or 80 mm)
  - Modules: turn on "Pharmacy" if you sell medicines
  - Owner account: your name, username and a 4-6 digit PIN

Remember the owner PIN. Without it nobody can change settings.


3. USERS AND ROLES
------------------
  Cashier : sell, hold bills, take customer payments, search items
  Manager : everything a cashier can do + items, purchases, returns, void
  Owner   : everything + settings, users, reports, backup/restore

Add users in Settings > User Accounts. Each person should log in with
their own PIN so the records show who did what.
After 5 wrong PINs, that user is locked for 1 minute.


4. DAILY ROUTINE
----------------
  Morning : Log in > Cash > "Open shift" > count the drawer cash and enter it.
  Day     : Sell on the Sale screen.
  Evening : Cash > "Close shift" > count the drawer cash and enter it.
            The app shows expected cash, counted cash and the difference.
            Print the Day-end (Z) report.

You cannot complete a sale until a shift is open.


5. SALE SCREEN (KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS)
-----------------------------------
  F1     Search item (type name, short name or barcode)
  Enter  Add the item / confirm
  F2     Change unit (e.g. tablet <-> strip <-> box)
  F3     Choose customer (needed for credit / khata sales)
  F4     Discount (one line or the whole bill, Rs or %)
  F5     Hold bill (customer will come back)
  F6     Recall a held bill
  F8     Payment (cash, credit, or both) - shows change to give back
  F9     Print the last receipt again
  Esc    Close a window / cancel

A barcode scanner works like typing + Enter: just scan.
Held bills stay saved even if the power goes off.

Credit (khata) sale: press F3, choose the customer, press F8 and put
some or all of the amount on credit. If the customer has a credit limit,
the app will not allow more credit than the limit.

Return or void: open the invoice, then "Return selected items" or Void.
Void needs a manager or owner PIN.


6. ITEMS
--------
Items screen > "+ Add Item" (or Insert key).
  - Prices are entered in rupees, e.g. 3.50
  - Base unit = the smallest unit you sell (tablet, piece, gram)
  - Packs = bigger units, e.g. "Box" with factor 100 means 1 box = 100 tablets
  - Opening stock = how many you have right now (in base units)
  - "Track lots" = keep batch numbers and expiry dates (medicines)

Many items at once: Items > "Import CSV". Click "Copy Sample CSV Format"
to see the columns. Bad rows are shown in red and are skipped.


7. PURCHASES (STOCK IN)
-----------------------
Purchases > New purchase: choose supplier, enter bill number, then add
items with quantity and cost. For batch items, enter batch no + expiry.
Saving adds the stock and updates what you owe the supplier.
Purchase return: open the purchase and press Return.


8. CUSTOMERS, SUPPLIERS AND KHATA
---------------------------------
Parties screen: add customers and suppliers, with opening balance.
  - Receive payment : customer pays you money they owe
  - Make payment    : you pay a supplier
  - Statement       : full history between two dates, printable
Positive balance = they owe the shop. Negative = the shop owes them.


9. PHARMACY (only if the Pharmacy module is on)
-----------------------------------------------
  - Sales automatically use the batch that expires first.
  - Expired batches cannot be sold without the OWNER's PIN.
  - Expiry screen: medicines expiring in 30 / 60 / 90 days.
    "Copy as WhatsApp text" to send the list to a supplier.
  - Want-book: items that are running low are added automatically.
    You can also add items by hand. Copy as WhatsApp text to order.
  - Dashboard shows near-expiry and want-book counts.


10. REPORTS (owner only)
------------------------
Sales by day / item / user, profit, stock value, low stock,
customer receivables, supplier payables.
Choose dates, then Print or Export CSV (opens in Excel).


11. BACKUP - PLEASE READ
------------------------
The app makes a backup automatically every day and when you close it.
It keeps the last 30 backups.

Power cuts, virus or a dead hard disk can lose everything. So:
  - Settings > Backup & Restore > set an "extra folder" on a USB stick,
    and/or press "Backup now" and copy the file somewhere safe every week.
  - Restore (owner only): Settings > Backup & Restore > Restore.
    The app saves a safety copy first, then restarts with the old data.


12. SETTINGS (owner only)
-------------------------
  - Shop information, language (English / Urdu)
  - Printer, paper width, print after sale: Automatically / Ask / Never
  - Allow selling below zero stock (off by default)
  - Users, modules, backup & restore
Light / dark mode: click the sun / moon button at the top of the menu.


13. PROBLEMS
------------
  Receipt does not print : Settings > Printer > choose the printer >
                           Test print. The sale is still saved; press F9 later.
  "Not enough stock"     : add a purchase, or check the item's stock.
  "No shift is open"     : Cash > Open shift.
  Forgot cashier PIN     : owner deactivates that user in Settings > Users
                           and adds them again with a new PIN.
  Forgot OWNER PIN       : there is no reset yet. Write it down somewhere safe.
